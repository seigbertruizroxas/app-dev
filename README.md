# Initial D (Anime Series)

**Initial D** is a classic Japanese street-racing anime series centered around *touge* (mountain pass) downhill battles and JDM sports cars.

> "It's not about the car's horsepower—it's about how you use what you have on the mountain."

---

## Why It Stands Out
- **Iconic Cars:** Features legendary rides like the Toyota AE86 Sprinter Trueno, Nissan Skyline GT-R, and Honda NSX.
- **Technical Racing Focus:** Highlights weight transfer, cornering lines, and drift techniques rather than just straight-line speed.
- **Memorable Soundtrack:** High-energy Eurobeat tracks paired with intense race sequences.

## Top Arcs / Stages
1. First Stage – Akina downhill rivalries and mastering the AE86.
2. Fourth Stage – Project D's expedition racing format.
3. Fifth Stage – High-stakes battles on unfamiliar courses.

## Quick Reference
- Main protagonist's car: `Toyota Sprinter Trueno GT-Apex (AE86)`
- More details on the series: [Initial D Overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_D)
